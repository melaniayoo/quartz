---
title: Bandwidth
---

In an amplifier or electronic circuit, bandwidth is the range of frequencies that the circuit can process properly.

$$
\text{Bandwidth}=f_H-f_L
$$

where:

* `fL` is the lower cutoff frequency
* `fH` is the upper cutoff frequency

For example:

```text
Lower cutoff: 20 Hz
Upper cutoff: 20 kHz

Bandwidth = 20 kHz − 20 Hz
```

A wider bandwidth allows the circuit to process a wider range of signal frequencies.
