---
title: Switching Converter
---

A switching converter changes one DC voltage into another using rapidly switching transistors.

It stores and transfers energy using:

* inductors
* capacitors
* diodes
* transistors

Switching converters are commonly used as efficient [[Voltage Regulator]].

# Main Types

## Buck Converter

A buck converter lowers voltage.

```text
12 V → 5 V
```

## Boost Converter

A boost converter raises voltage.

```text
3.7 V → 5 V
```

## Buck-Boost Converter

A buck-boost converter can raise or lower voltage.

```text
Variable input → stable output
```

# Advantages

* high efficiency
* low power loss
* suitable for large voltage changes

# Disadvantages

* more complex than a linear regulator
* produces switching noise
* requires careful circuit layout

# Key Idea

A switching converter efficiently changes voltage by repeatedly switching and transferring stored energy.
