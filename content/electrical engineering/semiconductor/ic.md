title: IC (Integrated Circuit)

An integrated circuit, or IC (chip), is a small electronic circuit built on a single piece of semiconductor material, usually silicon.

# IC, Chip, and Semiconductor

* [[Semiconductor]]
    * A material whose electrical conductivity can be controlled. Silicon is the most commonly used semiconductor material.
* [[Integrated Circuit]]
    * An electronic circuit containing many components fabricated on a semiconductor material.
* Chip
    * A common informal term for an integrated circuit or the small piece of silicon containing it.

## Memory and Processing ICs

These ICs store data or process information.

* [[NAND Flash]]
* [[SoC]]
* LPDDR
* [[MCU]]

## Power Management ICs

These ICs control voltage, current, charging, and power distribution.

* [[Voltage Regulator]]
* Charging IC
    * controls how electrical power is delivered to a rechargeable battery
* PMIC
    * manages and distributes power inside an electronic device
* Wireless Charging IC
    * controls the transfer of power without a physical cable connection
    * usually works using electromagnetic induction between two coils

## Connectivity and Interface ICs

These ICs allow electronic devices to communicate with other devices or components.

* Bluetooth IC
* Wi-Fi Module
* NFC Controller
    * Near Field Communication
    * manages very short-range wireless communication between nearby devices
* UWB SoC
    * Ultra-Wideband
    * a chip designed for short-range communication and precise position measurement
* USB-C Controller

## Sensor ICs

These ICs detect changes in the physical environment.

* Temperature Sensor IC
* Accelerometer and Gyroscope
    * accelerometer detects linear movement, and gyroscope detects rotation

## Audio and Input ICs

These ICs process audio signals or user input.

* [[Audio Amplifier]]
* Digitizer
    * converts touch or pen input into digital information (used in phones, and other touchscreen devices)

## RF ICs and Modules

These components generate, amplify, transmit, or receive radio-frequency signals.

* [[RF Transmitter]]
* RF Front-End Module
    * controls and improves RF signals between the antenna and the communication system
* [[Power Amplifier]]

# Key Idea

Each semiconductor IC is designed for a specific function.

In an electronic device, multiple ICs work together. For example, a smartphone may use:

* an SoC for processing
* LPDDR for temporary memory
* NAND Flash for storage
* a PMIC for power management
* Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC, and UWB ICs for wireless communication
* sensor ICs for motion and temperature detection
* an audio amplifier for speaker output
