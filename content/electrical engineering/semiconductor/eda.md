title: EDA Tools
---

EDA stands for **Electronic Design Automation**.
EDA tools are software used to design, simulate, verify, and analyze electronic circuits and semiconductor chips.

# Common Uses

* circuit simulation
* RTL simulation
* hardware verification
* synthesis
* timing analysis
* PCB design
* chip layout

# Examples

* LTspice
* ModelSim / Questa
* Synopsys VCS
* Cadence Xcelium
* Vivado
* Quartus
* Cadence Virtuoso
