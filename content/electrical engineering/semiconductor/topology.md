---
title: Topology
---

In circuit design, **topology** means the overall structure of a circuit and how its components are connected.
It describes the **type of circuit arrangement**, not the exact component values.

# Examples

Common amplifier topologies include:

* Common-Source Amplifier
* Source Follower
* Differential Amplifier
* Telescopic Cascode
* Folded Cascode
* Two-Stage Amplifier

Different topologies have different characteristics such as:

* gain
* bandwidth
* power consumption
* output swing
* noise
* stability

# Example

Two amplifiers may use different transistor sizes but still have the same topology if their connection structure is the same.

```text
Same connection structure
        ↓
Same topology
```

# Key Idea

**Topology = how the circuit is structurally connected.**
The topology determines the basic behavior and trade-offs of a circuit.
