---
title: Noise
---

Electrical noise is an unwanted signal that interferes with the intended voltage, current, or data in a circuit.

It can cause:

* incorrect measurements
* audio distortion
* communication errors
* unstable circuit behavior

# Common Sources

Noise can come from:

* switching regulators
* motors
* digital circuits
* power supplies
* electromagnetic interference
* thermal motion inside components

# Example

A sensor may produce a small voltage signal:

```text
Desired signal: 1.00 V
Noise:          ±0.02 V
Measured value: 0.98–1.02 V
```

The noise makes the measurement less accurate.

# Types of Noise

## Thermal Noise

Thermal noise is caused by the random movement of electrons inside components.

## Switching Noise

Switching noise is produced when transistors turn on and off quickly.

It is common in:

* switching regulators
* digital circuits
* processors

## Electromagnetic Interference

Electromagnetic interference, or EMI, occurs when electromagnetic energy from another device affects a circuit.

# Reducing Noise

Noise can be reduced using:

* capacitors
* filters
* shielding
* grounding
* shorter signal paths
* careful circuit-board layout
