## Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL)

**KVL:** The sum of all voltage rises and drops around a closed loop is zero.

Energy is conserved around a loop.

$$
\sum V = 0
$$

### Example

For a simple loop with one voltage source and two resistors:

$$
V_s - V_1 - V_2 = 0
$$

so

$$
V_s = V_1 + V_2
$$

This means the source voltage is divided across the resistors.

### When to use KVL

Use KVL when analyzing **loops** in a circuit.

KVL is the basis of **mesh analysis**.

---

## Intuition

- **KVL:** voltage gained and lost around a loop balances out.
- **KCL:** what comes into a node must go out.
