---
title: Overview of Power Systems
---

The electricity industry has 3 main activities:

1. **Generation:** Convert energy from sources such as hydro, fossil fuels, and nuclear power into electricity.
2. **Transmission:** Move electricity from generating stations to areas where it is needed, using high voltages for long distances.
3. **Distribution:** Deliver electricity from the power network to homes and other end users.

**Remember:** Electricity follows the path **generation → transmission → distribution**.
