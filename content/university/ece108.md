## Set Symbols

- $A \cup B$: **Union** — elements in $A$, $B$, or both
- $A \cap B$: **Intersection** — elements in both $A$ and $B$
- $A \subseteq B$: **Subset** — every element of $A$ is in $B$; $A=B$ is allowed
- $A \subsetneq B$: **Proper subset** — every element of $A$ is in $B$, but $A \ne B$

---
# Set Laws

## Commutative Laws

$$
E\cup F=F\cup E
$$

$$
EF=FE
$$

---

## Associative Laws

$$
(E\cup F)\cup G=E\cup(F\cup G)
$$

$$
(EF)G=E(FG)
$$

---

## Distributive Laws

$$
(E\cup F)G=EG\cup FG
$$

$$
EF\cup G=(E\cup G)(F\cup G)
$$
